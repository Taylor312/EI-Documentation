# Setup and integration

The main integrator track. Pages are ordered as a commissioning sequence: get the app installed and talking to the robot, configure the tooling and vision, wire up Modbus, teach and deploy a routine, validate it, then lock the station down for operators.

You can read out of order, but later pages assume the earlier ones are done. Every page states its prerequisites.

{: .warning }
Everything in this section can command a physical robot. Read [Safety]({{ site.baseurl }}/safety.html) before you commission a live cell.
